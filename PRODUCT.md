# Tark

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

UPSC aspirants studying current affairs and preparing for competitive examinations.

## Product Purpose

Connect current-affairs reading with syllabus context and exam practice. Help readers understand material and examine their preparation through practice and review.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The existing React application includes Intelligence, a Test Arena, a question library, review tools, a leaderboard and account membership. The application owns navigation and authentication. Quiz scoring, payments and ingestion remain server responsibilities.

The landing page's primary destination is Intelligence, confirmed by the user on 20 October 2026. Practice is a secondary entry. The user delegated composition and motion decisions while requiring continuity with Tark's branding.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve Tark's name, existing logo assets, navy/cream/gold/cyan colors and the academic, analytical character of the product. Reuse the existing Bricolage Grotesque, Newsreader and JetBrains Mono font system.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing source-linked current-affairs briefs and canonical UPSC question-bank content. Membership price is defined by the existing order service. Do not invent customer counts, live seat availability, performance claims or guarantees of complete factual verification.

## Product Principles

- Make article substance easier to understand than the surrounding interface.
- Use original evidence and distinguish demonstrations from live data.
- Offer a clear next action without duplicating the entire application navigation.
- Preserve accessible, responsive operation and reduced-motion behavior.
