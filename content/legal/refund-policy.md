# Refund, Cancellation & Lifetime Seat Allocation Policy

**EFFECTIVE DATE:** October 14, 2026  
**LAST UPDATED:** October 14, 2026

## 1. Philosophy: Try Before You Pay, Fair Recourse After You Pay

Tark does not lock its core mechanics behind a blind paywall. Every visitor can run the live timed $+2.00 / -0.66$ diagnostic and inspect the platform architecture on `tark.live` before spending a single rupee. However, if you reserve one of the **500 Lifetime Seats (₹399 paid once)** and find that the war room does not fit your preparation workflow, our refund policy is straightforward, transparent, and free of dark patterns.

---

## 2. The 7-Day No-Friction Refund Window

**2.1. Eligibility Criteria.** You are entitled to a **100% full refund of your ₹399 payment** if you submit a refund request within **seven (7) calendar days** ($168$ hours) of your transaction timestamp, provided that:
* **(a)** Your account has not been flagged for automated bulk-scraping or systematic downloading of the $2000\text{–}2025$ Observatory vault; and
* **(b)** Your account has not violated the Single-Aspirant Seat Restriction (credential sharing).

**2.2. Zero-Interrogation Process.** Within the $7$-day window, you do not need to justify your study habits or jump through retention hoops. A single email to `support@tark.live` stating your registered email address and Razorpay `payment_id` is sufficient.

---

## 3. Duplicate Payments & Technical Settlement Failures

**3.1. Unconditional Reversal for Failed/Duplicate Captures.** If your UPI account, debit/credit card, or net-banking account is debited more than once for a single seat, or if a payment is captured by Razorpay without your Lifetime Seat being provisioned due to a webhook timeout:
* Tark will either **(a)** immediately provision your Lifetime Seat upon verifying the Razorpay order ledger, or **(b)** issue a **100% unconditional refund** of the duplicate/unprovisioned charge, regardless of the $7$-day window.

---

## 4. Effect of Refund on the 500-Seat Cohort Ledger

Because Tark’s Founding Cohort is strictly capped at **500 Lifetime Seats**:
1. Upon initiation of your refund, your Lifetime Seat license is immediately revoked and your account reverts to unauthenticated/free tier status.
2. Your vacated seat is released back into the open inventory pool on `tark.live` (incrementing the open seat counter by $+1$) so another aspirant may claim it.
3. Once all $500$ seats are permanently claimed and the cohort closes, a refunded user cannot reclaim a seat under the ₹399 founding terms.

---

## 5. Refund Disbursement Timeline & Statutory Compliance

In compliance with the **Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020** and **Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Turnaround Time (TAT) directives**:
* **Approval & Initiation:** All valid refund requests are processed and initiated via the **Razorpay Refund API** within **24 to 48 hours** of receipt.
* **Bank / UPI Settlement:** Once initiated, funds are credited back to your original payment source (UPI ID, Bank Account, or Card) within **5 to 7 business days**, depending on your issuing bank's clearing cycle.
* **No Deductions:** Tark absorbs all payment gateway processing fees on refunded transactions; you receive the full **₹399.00** back.

---

## 6. No Recurring Billing (Zero Auto-Renewal Guarantee)

The **₹399 Lifetime Seat** is a **strict one-time transaction**. Tark does not create UPI Autopay mandates, e-NACH standing instructions, or recurring card subscriptions. You will never be billed a second time, and there is no recurring subscription to cancel.
