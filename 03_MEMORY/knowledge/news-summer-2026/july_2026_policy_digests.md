### India-Mali Bilateral Trade Expansion & West African Diplomacy

**Policy Mandates:**

> *   India's Duty-Free Tariff Preference (DFTP) Scheme for Least Developed Countries (LDCs) has been the primary driver of the trade surge between India and Mali.
> *   India will provide technical support to Mali to digitize its Certificate of Origin system.

**Trade Dynamics and Growth Trajectory:**

| Metrics | Values |
| --- | --- |
| **Bilateral Trade (FY 2025–26)** | USD 326.61 million |
| **Annual Growth (FY 2024-25)**     | 55% |
| **India's Core Exports to Mali**    | Pharmaceuticals, cotton fabrics, automobiles (two- and three-wheelers by Tata, Mahindra), and industrial Sonalika tractors |
| **Mali's Core Exports to India**     | Raw cotton, finished leather, cashew nuts, lead, gum arabic, and sesame seeds |

**Key Strategic and Sectoral Priorities:**

*   **Pharmaceutical Regulatory Alignment**: Mali committed to the fast-tracked, prompt registration of approved Indian pharmaceutical products to bolster its public health delivery networks.
*   **Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)**: Mali requested technical support from India to digitize its Certificate of Origin system, a move aimed at cutting down customs delays and modernizing its cross-border trade administration.
*   **Resource and Agrarian Interventions**: India urged Mali to reconsider its current export ban on shea-nuts. Both nations agreed to establish joint processing units for textiles, cotton, and mining.
*   **Securing Investments**: Amid regional transitions, Mali’s Prime Minister, Major General Abdoulaye Maïga, assured India of a safe, stable business environment to protect Indian nationals and private capital investments.

### India-Israel Bilateral Investment Agreement (BIA) & International Investment Law

**Policy Mandates:**

> *   The agreement moves away from old-style, investor-centric treaties to reflect modern principles of international investment law.
> *   It establishes a secure, transparent, and predictable legal climate for cross-border investments.

**Core Principles of the India-Israel BIA:**

*   **Investor and Investment Protection**: It protects foreign businesses from arbitrary, discriminatory state actions or unlawful expropriation.
*   **Retention of Sovereign Policy Space**: It explicitly retains sovereign policy space, allowing both governments to pass laws for legitimate public policy objectives.
*   **Alignment with Modern Indian BIT Model**: The BIA aligns with India’s revised strategy on Bilateral Investment Treaties (BITs), which uses strict definitions of “investment” and requires foreign investors to exhaust local legal remedies before seeking international arbitration.

### Rural Digital Infrastructure, Grassroots E-Governance & Telecom Regulation

#### India’s Largest Village-Level Telecom Network Performance Survey

*   The Department of Posts (DoP) and the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) signed a landmark Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) to launch India’s largest village-level telecom network performance survey.
*   The one-year project aims to systematically map and fix mobile connectivity gaps across rural India.

| Target Canvas                    | Description                                                                                                            |
|---------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| The Target Canvas                | The survey will audit and measure the live network performance of all four major Telecom Service Providers (TSPs)—Bharti Airtel, Reliance Jio, Vodafone Idea, and BSNL—across more than 5.68 lakh villages spanning all States and Union Territories. |
| Human Resource Deployment       | The project utilizes the Department of Posts’ network of over 1.40 lakh rural Branch Post Offices. The field tests will be carried out by Gramin Dak Sevaks (GDSs) during their routine daily mail and delivery operations. |
| The Tech Architecture            | Rather than relying on expensive tracking vehicles, GDS personnel will use a custom Android-based mobile application built by TRAI. This app runs background diagnostic tests to collect real-time data on signal strength, network latency, packet loss, and data speeds right at the consumer level. |

### Intellectual Property Rights, Biopiracy Mitigation, & Indo-Australian Technical Alignment

#### India-Australia Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) Access Agreement

*   The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) and IP Australia (the Australian Government’s intellectual property agency) officially signed a Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL) Access Agreement during the 3rd India–Australia Annual Summit held in Melbourne.

| Core Objective                     | Description                                                                                     |
|-------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| TKDL Core Objective                | It serves as a specialized defensive patent protection tool. Its primary role is to act as documented “prior art” to prevent global biopiracy—the erroneous patenting of traditional Indian knowledge by foreign corporate entities. |
| Scope of Content                   | The database contains scientific transcripts of over 5.2 lakh formulations and medical practices drawn from ancient Indian systems: Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, Sowa Rigpa, and Yoga. |
| Language Integration Strategy      | To make the database useful for global patent officers who cannot read ancient Sanskrit or Urdu texts, the repository translates these formulations into five international languages: English, German, French, Japanese, and Spanish. |

| Mechanics of the India-Australia Agreement | Description                                                                                     |
|---------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Global Scale Baseline                    | With this signing, 18 international patent offices now hold structured, NDA-protected entry keys to the TKDL database. |
| Proven Track Record                      | Globally, the TKDL has provided critical evidence to successfully revoke, amend, or reject more than 375 wrongful patent applications worldwide. |
