## [2026-08-12] Policy Updates: Gram Sabha Participation, Space Vision 2047, India‑SACU PTA, and CBDC‑Based DBT

### Gram Sabha Participation Enhancement
**Ministry of Panchayati Raj** released a National Study Report identifying four major causes of low participation:
- Daily wage loss & occupational conflicts limit attendance of landless laborers and migrant workers.
- Domestic burdens, social hesitation, male‑dominated discussions, and proxy governance curb women’s involvement.
- Infrequent presence of line‑department officials and weak grievance redressal erode trust.
- Inadequate advance notice of agendas and opaque GPDP allocations.

**Key Government Initiatives**
- **Capacity Building & Training**
  - *Revamped Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA)* – operating since FY **2022‑23**, provides orientation and thematic training to elected representatives, functionaries and disadvantaged groups (SC, ST, Women, Youth).
  - *Shashakta Panchayat Netri Abhiyan* – specialized leadership, administrative and legal‑literacy training for **Women Elected Representatives**.
- **Digital Public Infrastructure**
  - *Panchayat NIRNAY* – mobile/web app for end‑to‑end management of Gram Sabha meetings (agenda notification, photo‑proof of quorum, minutes, resolution publishing).
  - *eGramSwaraj Portal* – single platform for transparent preparation, tracking and execution of GPDPs.
- **Grassroots Democratic Engagement**
  - *Model Youth Gram Sabha (MYGS)* – school‑based simulation of Gram Sabha processes.
  - *People’s Plan Campaign (PPC) / “Sabki Yojana Sabka Vikas”* – annual (2 Oct – 31 Jan) structured participatory planning.
  - *Pre‑Meeting Mechanisms* – advisory to states to **mandate separate Ward Sabhas and Mahila Sabhas** before the main Gram Sabha.

> **Mandate:** The PPC shall “mandate structured, participatory planning sessions across all Gram Sabhas to finalize GPDPs” and states are required to hold **Ward Sabhas** and **Mahila Sabhas** prior to the main meeting to aggregate micro‑level and gender‑specific priorities.

### India‑SACU Preferential Trade Agreement (PTA)
**Overview** – India and the **Southern African Customs Union (SACU)** signed the Terms of Reference at Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi, initiating negotiations for a PTA – the first such ToR with an African bloc.

**Core Negotiating Architecture**
| Chapter | Focus |
|---------|-------|
| 1 | Trade in Goods & Market Access – tariff preference schedules |
| 2 | Rules of Origin & Origin Procedures |
| 3 | Customs Procedures & Trade Facilitation |
| 4 | Trade Remedies & Bilateral Safeguard Mechanisms |
| 5 | Sanitary and Phytosanitary (SPS) Measures |
| 6 | Technical Barriers to Trade (TBT) |
| 7 | Dispute Settlement Framework |
| 8 | Legal and Horizontal Provisions |

**Sectoral Strengths & Export Integration**
- **Pharmaceuticals:** affordable generics for Southern Africa.
- **IT Talent & Capacity Building:** digital infrastructure and skill development support.
- **Global Supply Chains:** integration of SACU firms into automotive, machinery, textiles and agro‑processing networks.

> **Mandate:** Negotiations will incorporate **Special and Differential Treatment (SDT)** to protect SACU’s Least Developed Countries and Small & Vulnerable Economies, ensuring equitable benefits.

### CBDC‑Based Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT)
The Government of India is launching a **CBDC‑based DBT** under **Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY)** in the Union Territories of **Chandigarh** and **Dadra & Nagar Haveli**. This follows pilots in Puducherry and Gujarat and will achieve full operational coverage.

**Key Features**
- **Programmability:** Digital Rupee tokens are coded to be spendable **only on foodgrains** at empanelled merchants, preventing diversion.
- **End‑to‑End Traceability:** Real‑time visibility of subsidy flow reduces leakages and cash‑handling costs.
- **Scalability:** Serves as a national proof‑of‑concept for integrating central bank digital currencies into other welfare schemes.

> **Mandate:** The programmable **Digital Rupee** tokens shall ensure that transferred subsidies are **exclusively spent on foodgrains**, providing end‑to‑end traceability and a scalable model for modern welfare delivery.

## [2026-08-17] Policy Updates: DRI Crackdown on SAFTA Misuse & India Guide to Grasslands (16‑17 Aug 2026)

### DRI Crackdown on SAFTA Misuse in Areca Nut Imports

**Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI)**, the apex anti‑smuggling agency under the **Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)**, dismantled a multi‑state network that imported areca nuts fraudulently under the **South Asian Free Trade Area (SAFTA)** agreement.

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Estimated customs duty fraud | ₹2,500 Crore |
| Arrests | 9 individuals (masterminds and facilitators) |
| Cash seized | ₹75 Lakh |
| Areca nuts seized | 160 Metric Tonnes |
| Regulatory action | License of the key Customs Broker suspended |

> **Mandate:** License of the implicated Customs Broker firm suspended.

**Key points**
- **Tax arbitrage:** 100 % Basic Customs Duty on areca nut imports, but 100 % exemption for SAFTA members meeting Rules of Origin.
- **Origin fraud:** Goods from non‑SAFTA nations were routed through Bangladesh EPZs to obtain false SAFTA certificates.
- **Impact:** Depressed domestic prices for growers in Karnataka, Kerala, Assam; direct revenue loss to the exchequer; erosion of trade integrity under SAFTA.

### India Launches Guide to Grasslands & Open Natural Ecosystems (ONEs)

India released its first **Guide to Grasslands and Other Open Natural Ecosystems (ONEs) of India** at a side event of the **UNCCD COP17** in **Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia**.

| Aspect | Details |
|---|---|
| Event | UNCCD COP17 side event |
| Location | Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia |
| Alignment | Land Degradation Neutrality (LDN) goals; International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists 2026 |
| Focus areas | Reclassification of ONEs, community governance models, biodiversity conservation, soil carbon dynamics |

> **Mandate:** Adopt the Guide to recognize and manage Open Natural Ecosystems as vital carbon sinks and biodiversity habitats.

**Key elements**
- **Paradigm shift:** Moves away from labeling open lands as “wasteland” to acknowledging their ecological functions.
- **Community governance:** Highlights traditional systems such as *Oran* (sacred groves) and *Gauchar* (village commons) for sustainable stewardship.
- **Biodiversity & carbon:** ONEs support species like the Great Indian Bustard and store significant soil organic carbon, enhancing climate resilience.
