# 📚 Physical, Human & Economic Geography (GS1)

> **Overview**: Geomorphology, Climatology, Oceanography, Indian Physical relief, River drainage basins, Soils, Mineral distributions, and Urbanization.

## 🎯 Core Syllabus Themes

1. **Plate Tectonics, Earthquakes, Volcanism & Landforms**
2. **Indian Monsoon Mechanism, ITCZ, Jet Streams, El Niño & IOD**
3. **Himalayan vs Peninsular River Systems**
4. **Soil Types (Alluvial, Black, Laterite) & Cropping Patterns**
5. **Mineral Resources & Industrial Location Factors**
